How it works

From waste to verified energy in five steps

01

Collection & segregation

Plastics and crop residue are sourced and sorted at the point of generation.

02

Cleaning & shredding

03

Fuel spec & QA

Before you commit

The questions buyers actually ask.

Specs, volumes, compliance paper and what a trial looks like — answered plainly.

Talk to our fuel desk →
01What fuel specification can you guarantee?+
3,200–4,200 kcal/kg GCV, chlorine under 0.8%, moisture under 12% — sized and densified to your kiln or boiler feed. Every batch ships against the agreed spec, not a typical value.
02What volumes can you commit to?+
Long-tenor MOUs typically start at 500–2,000 tonnes a month per site, with contracted tonnage ramped over an agreed trial window rather than promised on day one.
03Can we trial before signing anything long-term?+
Yes. A sample lot for lab validation, then a 30–60 day co-processing trial with our team on site, then ramp-up to contracted tonnage once your kiln accepts the feed.
04How do you prove compliance and traceability?+
Batch-level lab reports (GCV, moisture, chlorine, ash), source consents and recycler documentation, plus track-trace-audit CO₂ abatement reporting your ESG team can attach to a filing.
05Do you support us through burner changeover?+
We supply the SOPs, operator briefing and on-site changeover support, and stay through the first stabilised production runs.
06Where do you currently deliver?+
Tamil Nadu and Kerala industrial corridors first — Coimbatore, Palakkad, Chennai, Tiruppur — with regional sites for cement, power and FMCG off-takers.
