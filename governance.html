Policies — 04

Our policies.

These policies apply to every director, employee, contractor and partner acting on behalf of Enviro Enablers India Pvt. Ltd.

01

Business ethics & anti-bribery

We prohibit the offering, solicitation or acceptance of bribes in any form — including gifts, favours or monetary incentives — to influence decision-making. Employees must comply with applicable anti-bribery law, including India's Prevention of Corruption Act. We take a zero-tolerance approach to corruption and conduct every business relationship with professionalism, fairness and integrity.

02

Regulatory & environmental compliance

Our plants and sourcing units operate under the applicable environmental framework — including the Plastic Waste Management Rules and extended-producer-responsibility obligations, Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules, and state pollution-control consents — alongside labour, GST and company-law compliance. We maintain valid registrations and file returns on time, and treat regulatory approval as a licence to operate that must be earned daily.

03

Health, safety & environment (HSE)

Safety is non-negotiable in waste handling and fuel processing. We enforce site safety rules, PPE discipline, machine-safety protocols and fuel-quality testing so that what we deliver is safe for our people, our transporters and customer plants. We set clear HSE objectives, investigate every incident, and share lessons across sites.

04

Whistleblower & speak-up policy

Anyone — employee, supplier, transporter or customer — can raise a genuine concern about misconduct, malpractice or non-compliance without fear of retaliation. Reports are treated confidentially and reviewed independently. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious violation.

See something that doesn't feel right?

If you believe our standards are not being met, tell us. You can raise a concern through the contact form on our homepage or reach our leadership directly — confidentiality is respected and retaliation is never tolerated.
